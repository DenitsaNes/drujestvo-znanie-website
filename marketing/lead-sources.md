# Where to find more leads for the Haskovo seminar

Use these sources to grow your list beyond the 67 companies in the spreadsheet.

## 1. BUSINESS.bg – the main company directory
- Link: https://www.business.bg/en/c-239/haskovo.html
- Why: lists thousands of companies in Haskovo with phone/email/website.
- How: change the category filter to target specific industries: construction, manufacturing, trade, services, etc.
- Tip: also search nearby cities: Dimitrovgrad, Harmanli, Svilengrad, Lyubimets.

## 2. KorektnaFirma.com
- Link: https://www.korektnafirma.com/companies,4863,haskovo
- Why: lists Haskovo companies with addresses and EIK; good for finding manufacturers and employers.
- How: browse all pages; click “Виж визитка” to reveal phone numbers.

## 3. Haskovo Regional Business Profile
- Link: https://www.hs.government.bg/page/248-biznes-profil-na-oblast-haskovo
- Why: official overview of the biggest sectors and named companies in Haskovo region.
- How: use the company names listed under wine, dairy, meat, machinery, textiles, chemicals, then search each in BUSINESS.bg for contact details.

## 4. Jobs.bg / Rabota.bg / Zapлата.bg
- Links: https://www.jobs.bg, https://rabota.bg, https://zaplata.bg
- Why: companies that are currently hiring are active and have HR/payroll staff.
- How: filter by location “Haskovo”, note company names, then find their HR/ТРЗ contacts.

## 5. LinkedIn
- Why: you can directly find HR managers, ТРЗ specialists, chief accountants and business owners in Haskovo.
- How: search for:
  - “HR Manager Haskovo”
  - “ТРЗ Haskovo”
  - “счетоводител Haskovo”
  - “finance manager Haskovo”
- Send a short personal message with the registration link.

## 6. Facebook groups and pages
- Search: “Бизнес Хасково”, “Работа Хасково”, “Хасково бизнес”, “Счетоводство Хасково”
- Why: local business owners and accountants are active there.
- How: post the seminar link every 2–3 days and message group admins to ask for a pinned post.

## 7. Haskovo Municipality / Chamber of Commerce
- Haskovo municipality companies: https://www.haskovo.bg/companies
- Haskovo Chamber of Commerce (HCCI): https://www.bcci.bg/rcci/hcci/region.htm
- Why: municipal enterprises and chamber members are established local employers.
- How: ask the chamber to distribute your invitation to its members.

## 8. Google Maps
- Search: “фирми Хасково”, “производство Хасково”, “счетоводна къща Хасково”
- Why: shows phone numbers and sometimes websites.
- How: open each listing, save phone/website, then call.

## 9. Bulgarian Commercial Register (AJPES)
- Link: https://www.registryagency.bg/bg/registers/targovski-registar/
- Why: you can search by company name, EIK or region.
- How: use it to confirm a company is active and find its official address.

## 10. Yellow Pages / Golden Pages Bulgaria
- Link: https://www.goldenpages.bg
- Why: older but still useful for service businesses and manufacturers.
- How: search by city and category.

## 11. Local media and portals
- https://haskovo.net
- https://haskovo.com
- https://haskovo.info
- Why: local businesses advertise there; you can also buy a small ad or sponsored post.

## 12. Your existing network
- Past seminar participants
- Teodora’s professional contacts
- Clients of Drujestvo Znanie
- Colleagues, friends, family in business
- Why: warm referrals convert much faster than cold outreach.
- How: send a personal message asking them to forward the invitation to 2–3 relevant people.

---

## Recommended daily workflow
1. Pick 1–2 sources from this list.
2. Add 10–15 new companies to the spreadsheet.
3. Verify the phone/email (call the main number if needed).
4. Call or message 20–30 people from your list.
5. Update Status and Next follow-up for every contact.
